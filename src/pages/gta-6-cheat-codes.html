GTA 6 Cheat Codes

Radio Times and Red Bull list Story Mode button codes for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, but no standalone code list turned up in the GTA VI materials we checked at Rockstar Support or on the official game page, and we could not establish who first reported the codes.

This page has not performed any in-game verification.
