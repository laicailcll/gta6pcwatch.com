This page has not performed any in-game verification.

Why a reported GTA 6 cheat may not work

Published lists disagree about several unlocks and inputs. The comparisons below identify the exact difference to check before repeating a sequence. Each row compares published instructions. Rockstar has not published an official answer to these specific discrepancies in the materials reviewed for this page.

Reported cheat or inputWhat differs between the sourcesExact difference in the published entries

These comparisons explain why a single copied sequence can leave two unresolved questions: whether that save has unlocked it and whether the source gives the same inputs as the game. No result in the table above establishes that a code works on a particular patch.

Money, phone numbers, saving, and platform questions

Can I use the money cheat before finishing the story?

GamesRadar+ describes post-story access. The extra money requirement is disputed above. No pre-story bypass appeared in the Rockstar material checked.

Are there phone numbers to dial?

Red Bull reports directional inputs, not dial-in numbers. An official number list was not found in the Rockstar material checked.

What happens to saves and achievements?

Tracker warns that cheats disable saving and achievements. We have not tested activation or reloading. Save beforehand and read the game’s warning.

Does a console code work on Windows?

Rockstar lists PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The reported entries identify no Windows build or keyboard controls. Official Windows cheat controls remain unpublished.

Does a new patch settle a conflict?

Neither Radio Times nor Red Bull identifies a build for these entries. Publication dates cannot establish which patch, if any, resolves the conflicts.
