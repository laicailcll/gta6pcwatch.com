GTA 6 PC Release Date

Rockstar has not announced a GTA 6 PC release date. Its official product page lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Reports point to early 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed that window.

What Rockstar has confirmed

Rockstar lists GTA 6 as available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Its November 3 Newswire post announced console preloading on November 10 and named November 19 as the console launch date. Those dates apply to the console versions. Rockstar’s announcement

What outlets have reported

GamesBeat reports a first-quarter 2027 window. GameGPU reports March 2027 and adds a claim about GTA 6 Online launching alongside it. March falls within the first quarter. The shared window leaves the month and the proposed Online timing unconfirmed. The Steam post is an editorial guide from Video Games Chronicle.

What remains unknown

The official material checked for this page gives no Windows release day, PC system requirements, or details on whether story saves will carry over from consoles.
