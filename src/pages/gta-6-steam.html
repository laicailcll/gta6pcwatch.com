The two intervals measure different waits. Console → Steam starts at the original console launch; Windows → Steam starts at the first PC launch. These are historical comparisons, not a GTA VI forecast. GTA VI’s Steam release date remains unannounced.

  • GTA V: April 14, 2015 − September 17, 2013 = 574 calendar days; April 14, 2015 − April 14, 2015 = 0 days.
  • RDR2: December 5, 2019 − October 26, 2018 = 405 calendar days; December 5, 2019 − November 5, 2019 = 30 days.

The RDR2 PC announcement was published on November 5, 2019 and said Steam would follow in December. Steam’s own listing gives December 5, 2019. The dates therefore describe a PC release followed by a later storefront release. GTA V’s first PC release and Steam listing instead share April 14, 2015. These two records do not establish either an exclusive-store period or simultaneous Steam availability for GTA VI.
