GTA 6 Characters

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are GTA 6’s two protagonists, partners who rely on each other after a job goes wrong. Rockstar’s Trailer 2 introduces their shared predicament.

Officially Confirmed

Who is the main character in GTA 6?

Rockstar puts both Jason and Lucia at the center of the story. Its Trailer 2 page names them together and describes the trouble they face.

Jason and Lucia

Jason Duval

Jason served in the Army and later worked for drug runners in the Keys. He wants an easier life, and meeting Lucia gives him a reason to consider a different one. Rockstar names him in Trailer 2 and has a separate clip under his full name. Rockstar profile · Trailer 2 · Jason clip

Lucia Caminos

Lucia comes out of Leonida Penitentiary determined to make more careful choices and build the life her mother wanted. She sees a possible future with Jason. Rockstar names her in Trailer 2 and gives her a separate clip. Rockstar profile · Trailer 2 · Lucia clip

GTA 6 protagonists in Rockstar’s videos

Rockstar’s official video library lists the two protagonist clips separately from the numbered trailers. The cast entries below link to the named clips where Rockstar supplies them.

GTA 6 character names and supporting cast

This roster covers the people Rockstar names in its Only in Leonida character guide, including the people mentioned within other characters’ profiles.

Cal Hampton

Cal is Jason’s friend and one of Brian’s associates. He prefers staying home and listening in on Coast Guard communications. Jason has bigger plans. Rockstar profile · Cal clip

Boobie Ike

Boobie’s Vice City businesses include real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. At Only Raw Records, he and Dre’Quan are looking for a hit. Rockstar profile · Boobie clip

Dre’Quan Priest

Dre’Quan wants a career in music and books acts for Boobie’s club. He signs Real Dimez and sets his sights on the wider Vice City music scene. Rockstar profile · Dre’Quan clip

Bae-Luxe

Bae-Luxe performs with her longtime friend Roxy as Real Dimez. Rockstar gives the two a shared profile and video, and says the duo signed with Only Raw Records after an earlier hit. Rockstar’s Real Dimez profile · Real Dimez clip

Roxy

Roxy and Bae-Luxe have been friends since high school. Their shared profile describes how rap and social media helped Real Dimez build an audience. Rockstar labels the companion clip with the duo’s name. Rockstar’s Real Dimez profile · Real Dimez clip

Raul Bautista

Raul is an experienced bank robber looking for people willing to take on riskier jobs. His profile describes how his appetite for risk raises the stakes for his crew. Rockstar profile · Raul clip

Brian Heder

Brian runs drugs through a boatyard in the Keys with his wife Lori. His arrangement with Jason trades rent-free housing for help with local shakedowns. Rockstar profile · Brian clip

Lori

Rockstar names Lori in Brian’s profile as his wife and describes her involvement in the boatyard operation. That profile gives her first name and their connection. Rockstar’s Brian Heder profile

DWNPLY

Rockstar identifies DWNPLY as a local rapper who worked with Real Dimez on an early hit. The character guide gives that collaboration as the context for the name. Rockstar’s Real Dimez profile
