GTA 6 PC Requirements

Rockstar has not published minimum or recommended PC requirements for GTA 6. Rockstar has also given no PC release date. Its official product page lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The installation requirements in Rockstar Support are for consoles. Our GTA 6 Steam tracker covers current store availability and dated release announcements.
